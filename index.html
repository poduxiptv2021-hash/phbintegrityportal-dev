Privacy Policy

PHB's Integrity Portal is committed to protecting the privacy of all users. We adhere to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and all applicable Malaysian laws.

Anonymous submissions: No IP address, device information, or identifying metadata is collected or stored for anonymous reports.

Identified submissions: Personal data provided voluntarily is encrypted and accessible only to authorised IGU officers. It will not be disclosed to the subject of investigation without your consent.

Data retention: Case records are retained for 7 years in accordance with PHB's records management policy.

Whistleblower Protection Act 2010

Malaysia's Whistleblower Protection Act 2010 (WPA) provides comprehensive protection to persons who make genuine disclosures of improper conduct.

Key protections include:

  • Protection from civil and criminal liability
  • Confidentiality of the whistleblower's identity
  • Protection from detrimental action (demotion, harassment, dismissal)
  • Immunity from disciplinary proceedings

PHB fully supports and enforces these protections internally. Any retaliation against a whistleblower is a serious disciplinary offence.
